# Data Extraction Summary: 8 Included sMRI Studies

## Comparison Focus

This scoping review focuses on **within-diagnosis smoking comparisons** rather than between-group diagnostic comparisons:

| Comparison | Definition | Priority |
|------------|------------|----------|
| **Within-diagnosis smoking** | SZ-Smokers vs. SZ-Non-smokers | PRIMARY |
| **Diagnosis × Smoking interaction** | Does smoking affect SZ differently than HC? | SECONDARY |
| **Symptom × Smoking** | Brain-symptom correlations in smokers | TERTIARY |
| Between-group (SZ vs. HC) | General SZ brain differences | Context only* |

*Between-group (SZ vs. HC) comparisons are well-characterized in existing meta-analyses and not the focus of this review.

## Key Findings by Brain Region

### Prefrontal Cortex (DLPFC, lateral PFC)

| Study | Finding | Direction |
|-------|---------|-----------|
| Tregellas 2007 | Smokers with SZ had GREATER lateral PFC GM | ↑ in smokers |
| Schneider 2014 | Smokers with SZ had LOWER DLPFC volume (~2.5%) | ↓ in smokers |
| Yokoyama 2018 | Additive effect: SZ + smoking → reduced left PFC | ↓ in smokers |
| Qiu 2024 | Tobacco → positive GMV correlation in PFC | ↑ with tobacco |

**Summary**: Mixed findings. Early study (Tregellas) suggested potential protective effect; later studies show reduction.

### Hippocampus

| Study | Finding | Direction |
|-------|---------|-----------|
| Schneider 2014 | Smokers with SZ had LOWER hippocampal volume (~2.2%) | ↓ in smokers |
| Qiu 2024 | Negative GMV correlation with tobacco use | ↓ with tobacco |
| Musket 2026 | Smaller hippocampal volume with dose-response (pack-years) | ↓ in smokers |

**Summary**: Most consistent finding across studies. Dose-response relationship (Musket 2026) and longitudinal evidence (Van Haren 2010) support causal interpretation.

### Anterior Cingulate Cortex (ACC)

| Study | Finding | Direction |
|-------|---------|-----------|
| Jørgensen 2015 | Smokers with SZ had thinner left rostral ACC | ↓ in smokers |
| Qiu 2024 | Part of DMN abnormalities with tobacco | ↓ with tobacco |

**Summary**: Consistent reduction in ACC thickness/volume.

### Insula

| Study | Finding | Direction |
|-------|---------|-----------|
| Jørgensen 2015 | Smokers with SZ had thinner left insula | ↓ in smokers |
| Qiu 2024 | Negative GMV correlation in insula | ↓ with tobacco |

**Summary**: Consistent reduction in insula.

### Temporal Cortex (STG)

| Study | Finding | Direction |
|-------|---------|-----------|
| Tregellas 2007 | Smokers with SZ had GREATER right STG GM | ↑ in smokers |

**Summary**: Only one study; suggests possible preservation.

### Global Measures

| Study | Finding |
|-------|---------|
| Van Haren 2010 | Smoking did NOT explain progressive GM loss; heavy smoking (>25 CPD) may accelerate loss |
| Ringin 2022 | HC smokers: widespread reductions; SSD smokers: attenuated effect (interaction) |

## PRIMARY: Within-Diagnosis Smoking Effects (SZ-Smokers vs. SZ-Non-smokers)

| Study | Brain Region | Direction | Effect Size |
|-------|--------------|-----------|-------------|
| Tregellas 2007 | Medial frontal, STG | ↑ in smokers | Not reported |
| Van Haren 2010 | Global GM (heavy smokers) | ↓ progressive | 5-year follow-up |
| Schneider 2014 | Hippocampus, DLPFC, amygdala | ↓ in smokers | 2.2% hippocampal reduction |
| Jørgensen 2015 | ACC, insula, OFC | ↓ in smokers | Thinner cortex |
| Yokoyama 2018 | Left middle frontal, precentral | ↓ in smokers | VBM reduction |
| Ringin 2022 | Thalamus, pallidum | Interaction | No main effect |
| Qiu 2024 | Hippocampus, ACC, insula, temporal | ↓ with tobacco | Multimodal |
| Musket 2026 | Hippocampus | ↓ in smokers | Dose-response with pack-years |

---

## SECONDARY: Diagnosis × Smoking Interactions

| Study | Interaction Found? | Interpretation |
|-------|-------------------|----------------|
| Tregellas 2007 | Yes | Smoking moderated GM deficits (potential protection) |
| Van Haren 2010 | No | No significant interaction |
| Schneider 2014 | Yes (partial) | Non-smoker SZ ≈ HC; smoker SZ < HC |
| Jørgensen 2015 | Yes | Smoking effects specific to patients |
| Yokoyama 2018 | No | Additive (not interactive) effects |
| Ringin 2022 | Yes | SSD showed attenuated smoking effect vs. HC |
| Qiu 2024 | Yes | Tobacco × SZ interaction in PFC-symptom correlation |
| Musket 2026 | No | No explicit interaction reported |

**Summary**: 5/8 studies report some form of interaction, but direction varies.

---

## TERTIARY: Symptom Severity × Smoking × Brain Correlations

| Study | Scale | Brain-Symptom-Smoking Link |
|-------|-------|---------------------------|
| Van Haren 2010 | PANSS | Not specifically reported for smoking |
| Schneider 2014 | Yes (unspecified) | Not specifically reported for smoking |
| Yokoyama 2018 | PANSS | **Yes**: Smaller left PFC associated with more positive/negative symptoms; pack-years correlated |
| Ringin 2022 | Yes | Cognition also examined; interaction effects |
| Qiu 2024 | SBS | **Yes**: PFC GMV negatively correlated with symptom burden in SZ smokers |

**Key insight**: Two studies (Yokoyama, Qiu) show that smoking-related brain differences correlate with symptom severity, suggesting a potential mechanism.

## Methodological Notes

### Strengths
- Mix of VBM and surface-based (FreeSurfer) approaches
- Several multi-site studies with large samples
- Both global and regional measures

### Limitations
- Cross-sectional designs (except Van Haren 2010)
- Varying smoking definitions
- No standardized pack-year thresholds
- No psychological flexibility measures
- DTI excluded (see rationale in PCC_framework.md)

## Convergent Brain Regions (for Psychological Flexibility Hypothesis)

Regions appearing in BOTH literatures (see manuscript Table 3 for full comparison):

| Region | sMRI: Smoking in SCZ | Direction | fMRI: PF/ACT | Direction |
|--------|---------------------|-----------|--------------|-----------|
| **ACC** | Jørgensen (2015); Qiu (2024) | ↓ in smokers | Smallwood (2016); Aytur (2021) | ↓ post-ACT |
| **Insula** | Jørgensen (2015); Qiu (2024) | ↓ in smokers | Smallwood (2016); Lee (2023) | Mixed |
| **Prefrontal** | Schneider (2014); Yokoyama (2018) | ↓ in smokers | Smallwood (2016); Lee (2023) | ↓ post-ACT |
| **Hippocampus** | Schneider (2014); Qiu (2024); Musket (2026) | ↓ in smokers | — | Not examined |

**Note**: Hippocampus is the most robust sMRI finding but has NOT been examined in the psychological flexibility fMRI literature. This represents a priority target for future research.

**Caution**: Regional overlap must be interpreted cautiously—these regions are commonly reported across neuroimaging studies generally (base-rate problem).
